Evidence-Based ABA Therapy for Children & Youth

Empowering individuals with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) and behavioral challenges to gain independence, communicate effectively, and thrive in daily life.

Core Skills & Milestones Targeted

Our Board Certified Behavior Analysts (BCBA) and Registered Behavior Technicians (RBT) design personalized behavior intervention plans (BIP) that focus on:

  • ✓
    Functional Communication
    Verbal requesting, PECS, sign & AAC device usage
  • ✓
    Behavior Reduction
    Decreasing tantrums, aggression, elopement & self-injury
  • ✓
    Social & Peer Interaction
    Turn-taking, joint attention, conversation & cooperative play
  • ✓
    Daily Living Routines (ADLs)
    Potty training, mealtime tolerance, brushing teeth & dressing
  • ✓
    School Readiness & Transitions
    Instruction following, focus, coping with schedule changes
  • ✓
    Caregiver Training & Coaching
    Empowering parents with proven techniques in the home

Our Positive Reinforcement Philosophy

★ Play-Based & Child-Centered Learning

We use Natural Environment Teaching (NET) and Discrete Trial Training (DTT) embedded in fun, rewarding activities. Children stay engaged, motivated, and happy while making meaningful developmental strides.

Who Can Benefit?

Ages 18 months to 21 years diagnosed with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), ADHD, developmental delay, or emotional/behavioral regulation needs.
