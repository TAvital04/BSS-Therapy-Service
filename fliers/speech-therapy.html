Pediatric Speech & Language

Giving Every Child a Voice to Connect & Express

Individualized speech and language therapy delivered by licensed Speech-Language Pathologists (SLPs) to help children communicate clearly, confidently, and joyfully.

Areas of Specialized Treatment

Our speech pathologists assess and treat a comprehensive range of speech and communication disorders:

  • ✓
    Articulation & Phonology
    Speech clarity, pronunciation, and sound substitutions
  • ✓
    Expressive Language
    Vocabulary expansion, sentence structure, verbal expression
  • ✓
    Receptive Language
    Following directions, comprehension, concept learning
  • ✓
    Augmentative Communication (AAC)
    High-tech tablets, picture exchange (PECS), speech apps
  • ✓
    Pragmatic & Social Skills
    Conversational turn-taking, eye contact, nonverbal cues
  • ✓
    Feeding & Swallowing Therapy
    Oral motor weakness, sensory food aversion, chewing difficulties

Early Intervention Matters

★ Milestone Screenings & Parent Guidance

If your toddler is not babbling by 12 months, using single words by 16 months, or having trouble being understood by family members, early speech intervention yields the strongest developmental outcomes.

Who Can Benefit?

Children from age 1 through adolescence presenting with speech delay, developmental language disorder, childhood apraxia of speech (CAS), autism spectrum, or stuttering.
