Gross Motor & Mobility Program

Enhancing Movement, Strength, Balance & Confidence

Specialized pediatric physical therapy helping infants, children, and teens overcome motor delays, conquer gross motor milestones, and participate fully in play and sports.

Core Conditions & Skills We Treat

Our licensed physical therapists design engaging therapeutic movement programs focusing on:

  • ✓
    Developmental Gross Motor Milestones
    Rolling, sitting, crawling, cruising, walking & running
  • ✓
    Infant Torticollis & Plagiocephaly
    Neck muscle tightness, head tilt, and cranial asymmetry
  • ✓
    Gait Training & Balance Control
    Toe walking, in-toeing, frequent falling, stair navigation
  • ✓
    Muscle Tone & Core Strength
    Managing hypotonia (low tone), spasticity, trunk posture
  • ✓
    Coordination & Agility Training
    Jumping, ball skills, playground navigation & endurance
  • ✓
    Orthotics & Adaptive Equipment
    Evaluation for SMO/AFO braces, standers, gait trainers

Active, Motivational Therapy Sessions

★ Play-Infused Physical Rehabilitation

Children learn movement best when having fun! We turn therapeutic strengthening, balance beams, obstacle courses, and scooter board tasks into an exhilarating adventure that kids look forward to every week.

Who Can Benefit?

Infants, toddlers, children, and young adults diagnosed with cerebral palsy, muscular dystrophy, spina bifida, down syndrome, developmental coordination disorder (DCD), or post-injury rehabilitation.
