Sensory & Daily Living Development

Building Independence, Fine Motor Skills & Sensory Balance

Engaging, play-based occupational therapy empowering children to overcome sensory barriers, excel at school tasks, and master everyday life skills with joy.

Key Focus Areas in Pediatric OT

Our licensed occupational therapists create playful, customized therapy sessions addressing:

  • ✓
    Sensory Integration & Regulation
    Processing lights, sounds, textures, movement & body awareness
  • ✓
    Fine Motor Coordination
    Pincer grasp, finger strength, manipulating buttons & coins
  • ✓
    Handwriting & Visual Motor
    Pencil control, line orientation, scissor cutting & tracing
  • ✓
    Daily Living Independence (ADLs)
    Dressing, shoe tying, zippers, utensil feeding & hand washing
  • ✓
    Bilateral Coordination & Praxis
    Using both hands collaboratively, motor planning & obstacle navigation
  • ✓
    Emotional & Behavioral Self-Regulation
    Coping toolkits, calming strategies, focus & attention stamina

The Power of Sensory-Rich Play

★ Child-Centered Sensory Gym & Home Strategies

A child's "occupation" is play and learning. By guiding children through sensory diet routines, vestibular movement, and proprioceptive heavy work, we help them feel regulated, calm, and ready to learn.

Who Can Benefit?

Children diagnosed with Sensory Processing Disorder (SPD), Autism, ADHD, dyspraxia, developmental delays, or fine-motor school difficulties.
